# Rift extension and lake-onset literature compilation

Working record for EarthHistory (dev-docs scratch tier). Compiled 2026-09-14 by a
research worker from web search, publisher/Crossref/Semantic Scholar metadata and
open PDFs. All sources were accessed 2026-09-14.

Conventions used in every table:

- **Verbatim** = quoted from the paper's abstract or full text as retrieved.
- **Snippet** = a number reported by a search-engine summary of a page we could
  not open (paywall 403). Treat as unverified until the PDF is read.
- **Inference** = our own arithmetic or interpretation, always marked.
- Ages in Ma unless stated; radiocarbon ages are flagged `14C ka`, calibrated
  ages `cal ka`.
- Extension e from a stretching factor: e = W_present x (1 - 1/beta) (our
  arithmetic; requires the paper's present-day width W).

Publisher pages that returned HTTP 403 to this worker: lyellcollection.org,
agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com, academic.oup.com, sciencedirect.com,
pubs.geoscienceworld.org, ui.adsabs.harvard.edu (405). Abstracts for Lyell
titles were recovered through the Crossref API; AGU/Wiley, Elsevier and OUP
abstracts are not exposed there.

### A2. Notes on conflicts

- Jurassic beta on the ESB: Roberts 1993 (fault-population/forward model) ~1.15; Cowie 2005 quoting the Bellingham & White strain-rate inversion ~1.3. The two methods measure different things (brittle fault heave vs whole-lithosphere subsidence); the discrepancy is the well-known "extension discrepancy" and is why the ranges below are wide.
- Permo-Triassic beta: Odinsen 2000 gives 1.38-1.40 mean over the 120-125 km Horda-Viking transect, Roberts 1995 gives 1.4 for the Horda Platform and ~1.15 for the ESB. The whole-basin Permo-Triassic mean is therefore lower than 1.4.
- Timing: Roberts 1995 "probably early Triassic"; Phillips 2019 "late Permian-Early Triassic"; JGS 2024 "latest Permian" onset with c.10 Myr fault activity on most Horda faults but Sogn Graben activity through the Triassic. Jurassic phase 170-130 Ma (Cowie 2005), peak Late Jurassic-earliest Cretaceous (Ziegler 1992).

### A3. Our synthesis: cumulative extension across the northern North Sea (61 N transect, ESB-Viking Graben-Horda Platform)

**Status: our inference** (arithmetic on the numbers above; not a published curve).

Component estimates (our arithmetic):

- Permo-Triassic: Odinsen transect 120-125 km x (1 - 1/1.38..1.40) = 33-36 km; add ESB Triassic ~15% over ~75 km (Roberts 1995) = ~10 km. Whole-basin range **35-55 km**, central 45 km.
- Late Jurassic-Early Cretaceous: Odinsen 120-125 km x (1 - 1/1.08..1.13) = 9-14 km on the Horda-Viking transect; ESB 75 km x (1 - 1/1.15..1.3) = 10-17 km; axial VG 35-50 km x (1 - 1/1.25..1.5) = 7-17 km (overlaps Odinsen transect). Whole-basin range **20-35 km**, central 27 km.
- Total Mesozoic **55-90 km**, central ~72 km, on a present-day width of 200-250 km (Cowie 2005; Faerseth 1996 c. 200 km).
- Devonian: ~30 km (Mode I, onshore southern Norway; Fossen 1992 via Phillips 2019) plus unquantified Mode II shear-zone extension; not included in the Mesozoic cumulative below.
