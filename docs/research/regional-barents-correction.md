# Regional Barents material correction

**Accepted candidate:** `earthhistory-regional-barents-material-v1` supplies three source-constrained continental-material masks for the Cao coast polygons that terminate at 410 Ma: Timan–Pechora on plate 302, Novaya Zemlya on plate 373, and Franz Josef Land on plate 311. The correction represents inferred older substrate beneath modern cover and ice. It does not assert exposed land, a palaeoshoreline, relief, or uniform basement age. Surface state is `unknown` throughout.

Together these masks deliver 360,863.40 km², or 76.082% of the 474,306.92 km² summed scale of the 40 Barents target parts. With the separately compiled Svalbard correction, the regional delivery is 412,530.65 km², or 86.975% on that same scale. These ratios compare equal-area simplified unions with summed spherical target-part areas; they are scale indicators rather than exact set coverage.

## Sources, acquisition, and rights

The boundary source is Harrison et al. (2011), Geological Survey of Canada Map 2159A, **Geological map of the Arctic, 1:5,000,000**, [doi:10.4095/287868](https://doi.org/10.4095/287868). The reproducible acquisition queried ArcGIS layer 3, “Arctic Northern Europe-Russia GSC EN 1:5M Onshore Bedrock Geology,” for every feature intersecting the fixed 0–75°E, 60–85°N envelope. It retained all attributes and returned geometry in OGC CRS84, ordered by `OBJECTID_1`. The resulting 5,409-feature GeoJSON is 22,894,235 bytes with SHA-256 `94b081c306bb1dc169e32eb5928f17b75796dfede706053a3250707cfc557e72`.

The source is licensed under the [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada), with attribution to Natural Resources Canada, Geological Survey of Canada. The external store at `../EarthHistory-data/palaeomap-study/regional-corrections/barents/` owns a 160 MiB regional allowance inside the existing 4 GiB `palaeomap-study` bound. Its manifest pins the service, layer, open-data, licence, GeoJSON, and Crossref metadata responses by byte count and SHA-256. The acquisition refuses changed files in place and caps declared and streamed HTTP bodies before writing.

The spatial map labels do not establish material age by themselves. Each cohort therefore also cites an independent geological source:

- Kuznetsov et al. (2007), [doi:10.1016/j.gr.2006.10.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gr.2006.10.021), describes pre-Ordovician Timanide and northern Pre-Uralide complexes along the northeast East European Craton, including Timan–Pechora basement, with igneous ages from 700 to 500 Ma and distinct passive-margin, active-margin, and collision settings. This supports pre-410 material but warns against treating the whole mapped region as one uniform basement or one assembly event.
- Drachev (2016), [doi:10.1007/s41063-015-0014-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41063-015-0014-8), identifies the Western Uralides as Ordovician–Carboniferous shelf and slope strata of the Baltican passive continental margin, thrust over Baltica, with Neoproterozoic Timanian basement involved in the thrusts. This qualifies the Map 2159A `Urals foredeep, Western Ural Mountains` domain as a continental-substrate footprint while keeping the eastern oceanic sector separate.
- Ivanov et al. (2013), [doi:10.1016/j.jseaes.2013.02.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jseaes.2013.02.029), separates the western Ural continental rift/passive-margin development from later oceanic spreading and eastern Ural/West Siberian basement assemblages. It supports that same west/east distinction. Map 2159A independently assigns the additionally admitted `Urals folded region` records in the Timan target minimum ages of 542 Ma or older; the compiler accepts that class only when the record itself passes the 542 Ma threshold.
- Korago et al. (2022), [doi:10.1134/S0016852122020030](https://doi.org/10.1134/S0016852122020030), treats the pre-Palaeozoic basement of northern Novaya Zemlya, Late Vendian–Silurian successions, Caledonian tectogenesis, and later deformation. It supports older material beneath part of the archipelago while leaving the exact extent and affinity uncertain.
- Knudsen et al. (2019), [doi:10.1080/11035897.2019.1622151](https://doi.org/10.1080/11035897.2019.1622151), reports metasediments from the Nagurskaya drill core beneath western Franz Josef Land and detrital zircons spanning Meso- to Palaeoproterozoic ages, with a principal peak near 1.6 Ga. This is direct evidence for old detrital material and Caledonian metamorphism at one drilled locality. It is not proof of uniform Timanian basement beneath the complete archipelago.

Cao et al. (2024) model v2.4, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.13628813](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13628813), supplies the immutable coast targets, rotations, and a separately authored closed continental-boundary collection. The pinned `COBfile_1800_0.gpml` is 14,423,254 bytes with SHA-256 `cfcea20c5244613e53ad4b9cdf6411d79ff535c25af335b4fa6eb9251377d4bd`, under CC BY 4.0. Its Baltica plate-302 boundary is valid from 1,000 Ma, and its West/East Svalbard plate-309/311 boundaries are valid from 600 Ma. These boundaries are independent model extent witnesses, not observations of ancient exposure.

## Qualification by cohort

The compiler uses a spherical Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection centred at 40°E, 75°N on a 6,371,008.8 m sphere. It intersects exact source-domain labels with the immutable Cao target unions, insets the result by 1.5 km to guard source and target edges, and simplifies at 500 m with topology preservation. A 500 m native-overlap guard is removed from Timan and Franz Josef Land. Novaya retains its complete guarded mask because the shared renderer now gives visible native material visual and picking precedence wherever the later Kara Block overlaps. Every result must remain within both the selected source geometry and target to one square metre, retain a strict plate pose through 540 Ma, and remain below 8,000 vertices. Any active-native overlap outside the named Novaya/Kara exception remains a rejection.

- **Timan–Pechora, plate 302.** The admitted GSC footprint comprises `Russian Plate`, `Timan fold belt`, `Timan fold belt, Pechora Plate`, the western continental `Urals foredeep, Western Ural Mountains`, and only directly mapped pre-542 Ma records of `Urals folded region`. The source intersections contribute 179,177.86 km² from the original Timan/Pechora/Russian classes, 97,497.32 km² from the western Ural passive-margin domain, and 14,634.04 km² from the age-qualified folded-region units. Guarding leaves 284,651.53 km², and removal of 2,282.76 km² already represented by the native Kola Craton leaves 282,368.77 km². This is 87.421% of the 322,999.05 km² Timan target scale. The remaining 40,630.28 km² includes edge guards and explicitly excluded Pai-Khoi, Eastern Ural, West Siberian, and unrelated domains. The western Ural addition is a substrate inference beneath younger mapped cover; the younger rock itself is not backdated.
- **Novaya Zemlya, plate 373.** The admitted domains are exactly `Pai-Khoi, Novaya Zemlya` and `Urals folded region`. Intersecting GSC `ice` records contribute footprint only; the geological paper supplies the older-material basis. The source/target intersection is 75,919.83 km² and the complete guarded delivery is 69,178.75 km², or 87.770% of the 78,818.31 km² target scale. No source-qualified material is now removed merely because the native Kara Block appears later. The age sweep measures Kara overlap beginning at 460 Ma and reaching a maximum sampled 22,847.34 km² at 490 Ma; the correction draws first without writing depth and visible native Kara material wins both rendering and picking. The 9,639.57 km² residual is source mismatch and edge guarding, rather than a cross-phase omission.
- **Franz Josef Land, plate 311.** The admitted domain is exactly `Svalbard, Barents Shelf`; intersecting ice records again supply footprint only. The source/target intersection is 12,969.41 km² and the guarded delivery is 9,315.88 km², or 66.097% of the 14,094.24 km² target scale. The 4,778.36 km² residual is the source/target mismatch and edge guard. The broad mask is a low-confidence substrate inference from the coherent mapped domain plus the western drill-core evidence. It does not convert Jurassic basalt, younger cover, or ice into evidence of a pre-540 exposed surface.

The expanded Timan geometry has five components, no holes, and 513 vertices. Every feature records its selected domain names, target feature identifiers, source identifiers, processing metrics, pose witnesses, confidence intervals, and rejection conditions.

## Time, pose, and overlap

Material support is limited to `(410, 540]` Ma. The renderer treats `(410, 430]` as source-qualified model use and `(430, 540]` as an uncertain continuation. This split acknowledges that source geology supports old material more strongly than the exact older pose. It does not add an artificial material disappearance at 430 Ma.

Native-coast intersections were checked at 410.001 Ma, 411 Ma, every 5 Ma from 415 through 540 Ma, and at 430.001 Ma. Timan's Kola deduction is already represented throughout the checked interval, and Franz Josef Land requires no deduction. Novaya has zero active-native overlap through 455 Ma; from 460 Ma onward, every measured overlap belongs to the single pinned Kara Block feature. The renderer resolves that named alternative representation with native visual and visible-hit picking precedence while preserving the source-qualified Novaya material continuously.

The target geometries and rotations remain in Cao v2.4's palaeomagnetic frame, anchor plate 0. The regional map remains in modern WGS84 reference coordinates and is reconstructed only with the target plate's Cao rotation. No coordinates or rotations were transferred from another model.

## Reproduction and checks

`scripts/research/regional_barents_acquire.py` reproduces or verifies the bounded external snapshot. It also pins Crossref metadata for the two western Ural affinity sources. `scripts/research/regional_barents_compile.py` reproduces the selected domains, target intersections, edge guards, compact geometry, native-age sweep, pose witnesses, manifest, and validation record. The acquisition suite has three focused tests. The compiler suite has thirteen tests, including deliberate mutations of the western Ural admission, direct-old age threshold, ice interpretation, surface classification, geometry bytes, pose boundary, area metrics, the overlap-age sweep, Novaya cross-phase omission, and an unexpected native-overlap identity. The common Cao source-oracle validator also verifies source resolution, hashes, lifecycle, and public compilation.

Machine-readable evidence is in [regional-barents-validation.json](regional-barents-validation.json). The tracked inputs are [manifest.json](../../data/corrections/barents/manifest.json), [qualification-policy.json](../../data/corrections/barents/qualification-policy.json), and [barents-pre-540-material.geojson](../../data/corrections/barents/barents-pre-540-material.geojson).
